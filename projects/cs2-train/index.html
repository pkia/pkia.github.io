A plugin inside the game server reads the engine's own state every time you pull the trigger. Not a stopwatch. Not a kill counter. The physics.

01 · crosshair

Crosshair placement

The angle between your crosshair and the enemy's head at the exact moment you fired. Good pre-aim means you're already on target when the angle opens — the difference between a flick and a micro-correction.

02 · movement

Counter-strafe quality

Your velocity at the fire tick. If you shoot while still sliding, the game adds inaccuracy — the engine penalises you and so does the score. Train the stop, not just the shot.

03 · recoil

Spray discipline

Burst length and recoil index per bullet. The system knows when you should have reset your spray instead of dragging bullet 20 into the ceiling.
